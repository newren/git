Session title:
(Git Merge)²


Description:
Discussing improvements in "Git Merge" at "Git Merge", hence (Git Merge)².  This talk will discuss recent improvements in the merging machinery, which actually extends to several commands besides "merge".  It will include:

  * What new features have been enabled by the new merge
     backend
  * How a couple of the new optimizations involve trade-offs,
     and what the possible behavioral differences might mean for
     you
  * A brief story of my discovery process for finding the new
     optimizations added to the merge (and diff) machinery

Note that the optimizations will only be covered at an intuitive, high level; pointers to my previous blog posts will be provided for those that want to dive in deeper.


Notes:
I gave a talk on the merging machinery Git Merge 2020, but which focused on what optimizations were planned and why I was convinced they would work.  It was still very much a work in progress.  Time has passed, and those optimizations (plus some new ones) have been implemented, have passed review, have been improved, have been widely tested, and have even been documented in good detail in emails on the mailing list, in commit messages, and in blog posts.

So, I want to focus on other things that haven't been covered -- what the changes concretely mean for attendees beyond "faster" and "more correct", and the stories behind my discoveries.  I'll still discuss the optimizations, but at an even higher level with broad strokes.


Speaker tagline:
Modernizing, mechanizing, maintaining, and magnifying the merge machinery
